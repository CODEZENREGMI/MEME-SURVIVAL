Meme Survival Bosses

A boss every ten waves, a blackout at wave five, and a growing crowd of armed brutes in between. Here is everything that wants you dead.

Every 10 waves, a boss

Meme Survival throws a unique boss at you on every tenth wave, each with its own attacks, and the wave does not end until it is dead. In between, regular bosses join the horde more and more often.

WARLORD

WAVE 10 · 900 HP

Minigun, charges, summons. Good luck.

RAVAGER

WAVE 20 · 1600 HP

Twin cannons. Leaps. Half-dead, it only gets angrier.

BONA

WAVE 30 · 2600 HP

A mountain of black rock with fire inside. The lights go out when it comes. It charges, it slams the ground, it breathes magma.

KRAKEN

WAVE 40 · 1400 HP

Two miniguns. Eight tentacles. Spiral fire. Run.

TUNG TUNG SAHUR

WAVE 50 · 3200 HP

Tung. Tung. Tung. The log with the bat. Drum shockwaves, a boomerang bat, and a sahur call that brings the horde.

Wave 5: the lights die

On wave 5 the power fails on every map. You fight the whole wave by flashlight, and killing its boss lets something through. Don't look away.
